# Delete your FirstPass HQ account

You can delete your FirstPass HQ account at any time, in the app.

## In the app

1. Open FirstPass HQ and sign in to your club.
2. Tap **Delete account** at the bottom of the home screen.
3. Read what will be deleted, then tap **Delete my account**.
4. Tap **Delete my account permanently** to confirm.

If you signed in with Apple on an iPhone, Apple asks you to confirm once more. That also stops the app using your Apple sign-in; your Apple Account itself isn't changed.

This can't be undone. If you belong to more than one club, this deletes your account at the club you're in only; delete each one separately.

## What is deleted

Your profile, your squad and team places, your guardian links, your personal tasks, your messages inbox, and anyone you blocked in chat groups (with the club's record of those blocks). If you sign in again later, you'll need to ask to join again.

## What the club keeps

- **Your injury records,** where only the committee and admins can see them, for the club's keep period for injuries (7 years unless the club chose 3, 5 or 10), counted from when you leave, and then they are deleted. Clearance documents may go sooner, after the club's keep period for injury documents. Nothing is deleted while the club has removing old records switched off.
- **Under your name:** your code of conduct acceptances, any concerns you raised (unless you hid your name) and, if you coach or manage, your season KPIs and their reviews.
- **Messages you sent to a group,** and your posts in chat groups, stay with the people who received them.

If you're the only verified parent or guardian linked to a child under 18, their app locks again until another parent or guardian is linked, and the club's admins are told.

Deleting your account doesn't change your PlayHQ registration or your club membership.

## If the app says it can't be deleted

Now and then a club has to keep an account for a legal matter, or you're the club's only admin. The app then says so. Contact your club's admins.

## If you can't sign in

Ask your club's admins to remove your account, or write to us at pivtmedia@gmail.com with your name and your club, from the email you sign in with. We check it's you, ask your club to remove the account, and reply within 14 days.
