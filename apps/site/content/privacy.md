# FirstPass HQ privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

FirstPass HQ is an app that netball clubs use to run the club: messages, attendance, injuries, Working With Children Checks and child safety. This policy explains what the app keeps about you, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted.

## Who we are

FirstPass HQ is made and run by Matthew Brown ("FirstPass", "we"). You can reach us at pivtmedia@gmail.com.

Each club that uses FirstPass HQ decides who may join it and what it records, and may have its own privacy policy for its members. FirstPass runs the app and each club's database for the club. Questions about what your club records go to your club first. Questions about the app go to us.

## What the app keeps

Only what the club needs to run. Depending on your role, that is:

- **Your account:** the email of the Google or Apple account you sign in with (if you chose Apple's Hide My Email, the private address Apple gives us), the name Apple shares the first time you sign in with it, your name and preferred name, your role, title, squads and teams, and, if it differs, the email you registered with PlayHQ.
- **Your date of birth,** to know whether the club's rules for children apply.
- **Parent and guardian links** between children under 18 and their parents or guardians.
- **Working With Children Check details** for adults who work with children: card number, name on the card, expiry, application receipts and exemptions.
- **Health information:** injuries, physio updates, clearance letters and reports; medical and emergency details (emergency contacts and their phone numbers, conditions, allergies, medications, action plans); and daily wellbeing check-ins (sleep, soreness, stress, mood and a note).
- **Training and attendance:** training sessions athletes log (minutes and effort), attendance at sessions and games, and absences with their reasons.
- **What members write in the app:** group messages and who read them, survey answers, game goals and reflections, session plans and their photos, award votes, integrity training certificates, and concerns raised with the club.
- **Safeguarding records:** concerns, stand-downs, Screening Unit notices, parents' consents, code of conduct acceptances and policy acknowledgements.
- **A record of who opened a child's records,** and when, so parents can see it.
- **A notification token** for your phone, if you allow notifications.

The app does not use your location, your contacts or your phone's calendar. It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or rent your information. The app keeps a short technical log on your phone to help find faults; it never leaves the phone and has names, emails and health details removed.

## Why

To run the club: to sign you in, put you in the right squads and teams, send club messages, keep injured athletes safe and clear them to play, plan training, meet the law's screening rules for people working with children, and keep children safe.

## Who can see it

- **Club members see only what their role allows,** and the database itself enforces it. For example, a coach sees their own team's attendance and injuries; a parent sees only their own child.
- **For athletes under 18,** coaches, physios and the committee see the child's check-ins and injuries only after a verified parent or guardian consents in the app, and parents can withdraw that consent at any time. Parents' access ends on their child's 18th birthday.
- **Concerns** are seen by the club's Child Safety Officer, Complaints Manager and admins, never by the person a concern is about.
- **FirstPass** can technically read a club's data. We open it only to fix a fault, to make a copy the club asked for, or when the law requires it, and we tell the club's admins when we have.
- **Live streams:** if your club streams games with FirstPass Studio, an athlete's name and game stats are shown only with consent, given by a verified parent for an athlete under 18.
- Information isn't shared outside the club except with the Police or the sport's governing body when a child's safety requires it, with the Working with Children Screening Unit to check a card, or where the law requires.

## The app is not social media

Members can't message each other privately or reply to messages. There are no feeds, profile pictures, likes or following. Messages go from coaches, managers, the committee and admins to whole squads, teams or roles, and a parent gets a copy of every message to or from their child.

## Where it is kept

Each club has its own database, separate from every other club, hosted by Supabase on Amazon Web Services in Sydney, Australia. It is encrypted on disk and on its way between the app and the database.

These services help us run the app:

- **Supabase** hosts each club's database, files and sign-in.
- **Google** signs you in when you choose Continue with Google. The app receives your Google account's email and name, and a link to your Google profile picture, which the app never shows or uses.
- **Apple** signs you in when you choose Continue with Apple on an iPhone or iPad. The app receives your email or a private Apple address, and your name the first time. When you delete your account, the app asks Apple to stop the app's access to your Apple account.
- **Expo, Apple and Google** deliver notifications to your phone. Each notification's title and text (which can include a person's name, never injury details) and your phone's notification token pass through them.

Google and Apple sign-in and the notification services are run from outside Australia, so your Google or Apple account's email and name at sign-in, and each notification's title, text and token, are handled by those companies outside Australia under their own privacy terms. The records your club keeps are stored in its database and files in Sydney.

## How long it is kept

Each club chooses how long it keeps each kind of record, and nothing is removed until the club switches on removing old records. The app's recommended periods for the main kinds of record are:

- daily check-ins and training sessions: 1 year;
- attendance, absences and session plans: 1 year;
- game goals and reflections: 1 year;
- message text: 3 years, or 7 years when a child sent or received it;
- injury letters and reports: 1 year after the injury is cleared;
- injuries: 7 years after they are cleared, or after the athlete leaves the club;
- medical and emergency details: 2 years after their last yearly check;
- the record of who opened a child's records: 7 years;
- surveys: 3 years after they close for club surveys, 2 years for squad and team surveys, and 6 months for quick team polls;
- who voted for whom in awards: 1 year after the award closes;
- integrity training certificates: 1 year;
- safeguarding records: 30 years after each one ended;
- concerns: never removed.

Your account and profile are kept until you delete your account or the club removes you.

## Children

FirstPass HQ is not for children under 13. The join step turns away anyone under 13, and if we learn that a child under 13 has signed in, their sign-in can be deleted.

We collect from a child only what is listed above for their role, only to run the club, and never for advertising or tracking. Athletes under 18 can't use the app until the club links a verified parent or guardian to them. Parents see their child's records, give or withdraw consents, and get a copy of every message to or from their child.

## Your choices

- **See and correct your details** in the app under Your details.
- **Turn off notifications** in Your details, or in your phone's settings.
- **Delete your account** in the app. See [Delete your account](/delete-account) for what is deleted and what the club keeps.
- **Ask for a copy** of what the club holds about you, or ask for a correction, through your club's admins or by writing to us at pivtmedia@gmail.com.

## If something goes wrong

If we learn that a club's data may have been seen, changed or lost by someone who shouldn't have, we tell the club's admins within 72 hours of finding out and help the club decide who else to tell.
