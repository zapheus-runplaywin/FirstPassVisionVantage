# FirstPass HQ support

FirstPass HQ is the app netball clubs use to run the club. Here is how to get help.

## Getting into the app

1. Open the app and enter your club's code. Your club gives you the code, or a link that fills it in.
2. Tap Continue with Google and choose your Google account.
3. The first time, tap Request to join and fill in your details. A club admin approves you and puts you in your squad or team.

If you are under 18, the app stays locked until the club links your parent or guardian to you. Share the link code the app shows you with them.

## Questions about your club

Your role, your squad or team, the club password, approvals, and what the club records about you are all looked after by your club's admins. Ask them first.

## Questions about the app

Write to us at [SUPPORT EMAIL]. Tell us your club, what you were doing and what happened. Please don't send injury or medical details by email.

To raise a concern about a child's safety, use Raise a concern in the app, or contact your club's Child Safety Officer. If a child is in danger, call 000.

## Deleting your account

Delete account is at the bottom of the home screen. See [Delete your account](/delete-account) for the steps and for what the club keeps.
